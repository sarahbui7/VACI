Vietnamese American Community in Iowa

VACI Lions

We're the lion dance team representing the Vietnamese American Community in Iowa. Rooted in tradition, we perform to ward off bad luck, welcome good fortune, and celebrate our culture with the community.

Open to enthusiastic beginners and experienced dancers ages 12 and up — no experience needed. We train at the VACI center every Sunday at 2:00 PM and Monday at 5:00 PM, and perform throughout the year at events and festivals.
