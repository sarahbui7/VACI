Vietnamese American Community in Iowa

VACI Lions

We're the lion dance team representing the Vietnamese American Community in Iowa. Rooted in tradition, we perform to ward off bad luck, welcome good fortune, and celebrate our culture with the community.

We are open to enthusiastic beginners and experienced dancers ages 12 and up, no previous experience needed. We train at the VACI center every Sunday at 2:00 PM and Monday at 5:00 PM, and perform throughout the year at events and festivals.

Where You'll See Us

We perform throguhout the whole year for many different events.

01

Holidays

These would include the Lunar New Year/Tết celebrations or Mid Autumn/Moon festival.

02

Weddings & Grand Openings

When requested, we can perform in a more traditional setting for people's weddings and grand opening ceremonies.

03

Cultural & School Events

We love performing for these to show off our culture. This helps get more people interest in lion dancing and could get us more members!

04

VACI Events

Since we are based from the organization Vietnmaese American Community in Iowa, we often participate in their events and activities when needed. This helps bring in more attraction for our community and our lion dance team!
