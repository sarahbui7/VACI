Featured Photo Slideshow

Up to 5 photos, all 4:3. On the site these fade from one to the next in the box next to the intro text. You don't need to fill all 5 — it'll just cycle through whichever ones are set.

Recent Photos strip

The 4 scrolling photos on the home page, all 4:3.

What We Do

Upcoming Events

These show up on the "Upcoming Events" page, in order.

Past Events

These show up on the "Past Events" page grouped into a tab per year. Give each event a year below so it lands in the right tab — if you leave it blank, the site will try to guess the year from the date field.

Photo Gallery

Add as many photos as you like. Visitors click a photo to see it full-size with the description you write. Pick a ratio for each photo — 4:3, 1:1 (square), or 3:4 — they'll all display at the same height on the gallery page.
